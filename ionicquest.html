      Solubility of salts in water

      CompoundsSoluble?
      Sodium, potassium and ammonium saltsAll soluble
      NitratesAll soluble
      Chlorides and iodidesSoluble, except silver and lead(II)
      SulfatesSoluble, except barium, calcium and lead(II)
      CarbonatesInsoluble, except sodium, potassium and ammonium
      Hydroxides and oxidesInsoluble, except sodium, potassium and ammonium (calcium hydroxide is slightly soluble)

      Also: dilute acids and alkalis are (aq). Water is (l). Metals are (s). H2 and CO2 are (g).
